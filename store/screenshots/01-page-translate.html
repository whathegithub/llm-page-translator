语言

Language · 正在原位翻译

语言是结构化的沟通系统。它将声音、手势或文字组织成可共享的意义，让人能够交流想法、记忆和计划。

大多数自然语言同时具备词汇和语法。词汇提供可以指代事物的符号，语法则决定这些符号如何组合成更大的表达。

Writing systems make it possible to record speech across time. A page can keep headings, links, and buttons in place while only the readable text is replaced.

Machine translation works best when it preserves names, numbers, and the original tone, instead of rebuilding the layout from scratch.
